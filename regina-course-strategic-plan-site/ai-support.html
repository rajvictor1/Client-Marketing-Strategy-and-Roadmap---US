What AI Will Handle

Four workstreams where AI removes repetitive production and routing work.

What AI Will Handle

Content engine

  • Turn one lesson or voice note into article, newsletter, and social drafts.
  • Repurpose long-form content into Shorts, Reels, carousels, and quotes.
  • Suggest titles, headings, FAQs, and metadata for SEO/GEO.

Email and funnel

  • Welcome, diagnostic, workshop, launch, and alumni sequences.
  • Segment subscribers by source and behavior.
  • Send reminders and follow-ups after live events.

Demand generation

  • Run the Memoir Readiness Diagnostic and deliver personalized results.
  • Manage webinar registration, reminders, attendance, and replay follow-up.
  • Track partner links and attribute leads.

Operations and reporting

  • Summarize applications and flag fit issues.
  • Confirm payment, send onboarding, and assign cohorts.
  • Build dashboards and weekly decision briefs.

What Stays Human

  • Live teaching and personal expertise.
  • Manuscript feedback and publishing advice.
  • Participant selection and admissions.
  • Final approval of all public content.
  • Partner relationships and introductions.
  • Sensitive communication, complaints, and refunds.

Safeguards

  • Every automation defines: Trigger → Data → Decision → Tool → Action → Verification → Human escalation → Log → Metric.
  • Medium-risk actions (public drafts, campaign variations) require human approval before publishing.
  • High-risk actions (admissions, sensitive outreach, refunds, testimonials) require human decision.
  • Prohibited: impersonation, unapproved manuscript use, deceptive testimonials, protected-trait inference.
  • Audit trail records workflow version, input source, output, approval status, destination, result, and errors.

How We Prove It

We do not claim savings until we measure them. The first 30-day pilot tracks:

  • Baseline manual hours per workstream.
  • AI first-pass approval rate.
  • Average revision size and time.
  • Factual-error and off-brand rates.
  • Actual hours saved per approved asset.

Recommended First Pilot

Start with one contained workflow for 30 days:

  1. Regina shares one teaching idea or voice note.
  2. AI drafts one article + one newsletter + three social posts.
  3. Regina or her assistant edits and approves.
  4. Approved content is scheduled and published.
  5. We measure time saved, approval rate, and engagement.

Only expand to the diagnostic, email, and course-operations automations after this first workflow proves reliable.
