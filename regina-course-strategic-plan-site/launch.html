Launch Operating Sequence

Course strategy → Demand validation → Pilot launch → Enrollment → Delivery → Marketing → Improvement

Phase 1: Course strategy (Week 1)

  • Confirm audience, promise, curriculum, and $1,295 price with Regina.
  • Audit existing course materials and IP ownership.
  • Define success metrics.

Phase 2: Demand validation (Weeks 2–4)

  • Build a waitlist landing page.
  • Send announcement to Regina's email list and LinkedIn.
  • Publish 5–7 organic pieces about memoir writing and publishing.
  • Collect waitlist signups and questions.

Phase 3: Pilot launch (Weeks 5–6)

  • Open applications.
  • Run 4-email launch sequence to waitlist.
  • Host one free Q&A or mini-workshop.
  • Review applications manually.

Phase 4: Enrollment (Week 7)

  • Send acceptances and payment links.
  • Onboard the founding cohort.

Phase 5: Delivery (Weeks 8–13)

  • Run six weekly live sessions.
  • Collect weekly feedback and testimonials.
  • Track attendance, completion, and satisfaction.

Phase 6: Marketing (Weeks 14–16)

  • Use testimonials and results to promote next cohort.
  • Open public enrollment at $1,695.

Phase 7: Improvement (ongoing)

  • Refine curriculum, pricing, and automation based on data.
